Frequently Asked Questions

Are there any hidden setup fees?

No. The one-time setup fee for your chosen tier is disclosed upfront and covers your site build and profile configuration. There are no additional hidden charges beyond your monthly subscription.

Is ad spend included in the monthly fee?

No. Your monthly subscription covers our management and campaign work. Any Google or Meta ad spend on Growth and Lead tiers is billed separately and directly to your own ad account, so you always see exactly where your budget goes.

Do I need to sign a long-term contract?

No. All packages run on a flexible month-to-month basis. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your subscription at any time.

How fast can my site go live?

Most Foundation and Growth tier sites are designed, built, and deployed within 7 business days of receiving your business details and onboarding voice note.
